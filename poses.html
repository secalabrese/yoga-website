Six Beginner-Friendly Yoga Poses

Below is a collection of simple yoga poses suitable for beginners.

Enjoy this calming meditation track as you explore the beginner poses below.

Warrior II (Virabhadrasana II)

A strong standing pose that builds leg strength, opens the hips, and improves stability. Ideal for developing focus and grounding.

Easy Pose (Sukhasana)

A comfortable seated position used for meditation and steady breathing. Encourages calmness and lengthens the spine.

Tree Pose (Vriksasana)

A balancing posture that strengthens the legs and improves focus. Hands may rest together at the heart center to enhance grounding.

Triangle Pose (Trikonasana)

A gentle side-body stretch that lengthens the spine and opens the chest. Helps improve stability and posture.

Upward-Facing Dog (Urdhva Mukha Svanasana)

A gentle backbend that strengthens the arms and opens the chest. Promotes energizing breath and spinal flexibility.

Standing Backbend (Anuvittasana)

A beginner-friendly heart-opening pose that increases spine mobility and encourages confident, open posture.
